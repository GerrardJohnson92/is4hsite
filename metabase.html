Metabase Features Guide for IS4H Bahamas

What is Metabase?

Metabase is the centralized analytics platform for IS4H Bahamas. It empowers Ministry of Health and Wellness teams to explore, analyse, and visualize real-time health data from systems like BahamasEMR+, SIP+, and the Bahamas National Patient Portal—without needing to write code.

Goal: Turn health data into actionable insights that improve service delivery, planning, and policy decisions across The Bahamas.

Key Features

  • 1. Simple, No-Code Data Exploration
    • Use point-and-click tools to explore data without SQL knowledge.
    • Filter, group, and summarize data by date, clinic, district, diagnosis, etc.
    • Ideal for clinic managers, health administrators, and program officers.
    • Example: “How many antenatal patients visited Gambier Clinic last month?” → Select table → apply filters → view results.
  • 2. Powerful Visualizations
    • Create bar charts, pie charts, heat maps, trend lines, and more.
    • Visuals are auto-suggested based on your query.
    • Export charts for presentations, reports, or briefings.
    • Helps transform raw numbers (e.g. immunization rates) into compelling visuals.
  • 3. Dynamic Dashboards
    • Combine multiple visuals into a dashboard for a holistic view.
    • Add interactive filters (e.g. by date, island, service type).
    • Drill down from summary stats to individual clinic data or patient flows.
    • Example: A “Family Islands Health Dashboard” showing outpatient visits, medication stock, and referral trends.
  • 4. Email Alerts & Scheduled Reports
    • Set up data alerts (e.g. when stock falls below a threshold).
    • Subscribe key staff to weekly or monthly dashboards.
    • Ensures real-time visibility without needing to log in every day.
  • 5. Collections & Access Permissions
    • Dashboards and reports can be grouped into Collections (e.g. CNCDs, BEMR, SIP+).
    • Role-based access ensures the right data is seen by the right people.
    • Staff can request access from Metabase admins via the IS4H Helpdesk.
  • 6. Integration with IS4H Data Sources
    • Connected to: BahamasEMR+ (clinical visits, diagnoses, vitals), SIP+ (maternal and child health indicators), Bahamas National Patient Portal, Facility readiness & staffing assessments.
    • New data is synced regularly for up-to-date insights.

Security & Governance

  • Users are assigned Viewer, Editor, or Admin roles.
  • Access is limited to approved collections and datasets.
  • All dashboards are aligned with the IS4H Data Governance Framework.
