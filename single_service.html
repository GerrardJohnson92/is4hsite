BahamasEMR+ – Features Guide

The BahamasEMR+ is a customized electronic medical records (EMR) system designed to modernize and streamline healthcare delivery in The Bahamas. As a localized version of OpenEMR, BahamasEMR+ ensures efficient patient data management, enhanced clinical workflows, and seamless coordination between healthcare providers—laying the foundation for a fully digitalized public health system.

Key Features of BahamasEMR+

  • Centralized Patient Records
    • One Patient, One Record: Unified health record accessible across facilities.
    • Medical History, Allergies, and Chronic Condition Tracking.
  • Streamlined Clinical Workflows
    • Digital Documentation, Clinical Forms, and Automated Notes.
  • Appointment & Scheduling Management
    • Integrated Booking System with Automated Reminders.
  • Medication & Prescription Management
    • Future support for E-Prescribing and Drug Interaction Alerts.
  • Lab & Diagnostic Test Integration
    • Lab Orders, Imaging, and Results Tracking (Future Phases).
  • Billing & Insurance Integration
    • DigiPay Integration, Insurance Verification, and Fee Schedules.
  • Secure & Compliant Data Management
    • Role-Based Access, Encryption, and Audit Logs.
  • Reporting & Analytics
    • Custom Dashboards via Metabase and Health Trend Reports.

Benefits of BahamasEMR+

  • ✔️ Enhances Patient Care through updated, accessible records
  • ✔️ Reduces Admin Burden with automated workflows
  • ✔️ Improves Clinical Efficiency via digital scheduling and tracking
  • ✔️ Strengthens Data Security with advanced protocols
  • ✔️ Enables Evidence-Based Healthcare through analytics

The BahamasEMR+ is revolutionizing healthcare in The Bahamas. By replacing paper-based systems with an intuitive digital platform, BEMR+ enhances outcomes, supports providers, and ensures seamless national health data management.

For more information, contact is4h@bahamas.gov.bs.
