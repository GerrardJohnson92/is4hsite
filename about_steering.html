About the IS4H Bahamas Steering Committee

The Ministry of Health and Wellness established a National IS4H Steering Committee and Technical Committee to ensure coordination and collaboration among national stakeholders and partners. The IS4H Steering Committee provides strategic guidance and facilitates organizational collaboration and support for the activities and products identified in the IS4H Bahamas Plan of Action.

The IS4H Technical Committees provide technical advice and guidance to the IS4H Steering Committee, and guide the execution of technical activities and products, such as a national health information exchange architecture and IT standards, data governance and standards, policy development and alignment, and change management to support digital health transformation of the health system.

IS4H Bahamas is transforming care with smarter, faster, and more connected solutions. Your health, connected!
