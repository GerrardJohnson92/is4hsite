Bahamas National Patient Portal (BNPP) – Features Guide

The Bahamas National Patient Portal (BNPP) is a secure, user-friendly digital platform designed to empower patients by providing convenient access to their health information while enabling efficient communication with healthcare providers. This system is a key component of The Bahamas’ digital health transformation, ensuring seamless access, security, and better patient outcomes.

Key Features of BNPP

  1. Secure Patient Access
    • 🔹 Personalized Login with multi-factor authentication
    • 🔹 Data Protection with compliance-grade security
  2. Personal Health Records (PHR)
    • 🔹 Comprehensive Medical History, including diagnoses and allergies
    • 🔹 Medication & Prescription Details (future update)
    • 🔹 Immunization Records including COVID-19
  3. Appointment Management
    • 🔹 Online Scheduling & Rescheduling
    • 🔹 SMS or Email Reminders
  4. Lab & Test Results Access
    • 🔹 Real-Time Lab Results Access
    • 🔹 Provider Notes & Recommendations
  5. Communication & Messaging
    • 🔹 Secure Messaging for follow-ups
    • 🔹 Telehealth Integration (future feature)
  6. Health & Wellness Tracking
    • 🔹 Personalized Health Dashboard
    • 🔹 Future Integration with Fitness Wearables
  7. Billing & Insurance Information
    • 🔹 E-Billing & Payment Tracking
    • 🔹 Insurance Claim & Coverage Information
  8. Emergency & Caregiver Access
    • 🔹 Emergency Contact & Medical Directive Access
    • 🔹 Authorized Caregiver Account Viewing

Benefits of BNPP

  • ✔️ Empowers Patients with control over health data
  • ✔️ Enhances Efficiency by reducing paperwork
  • ✔️ Improves Communication with providers
  • ✔️ Ensures Continuity of Care with real-time access
  • ✔️ Supports Data-Driven Decisions for better outcomes

The Bahamas National Patient Portal is a transformative step toward modernizing healthcare in The Bahamas. By providing secure, real-time access to essential health information, BNPP is improving patient care, streamlining workflows, and enhancing public health management.

For more information, contact is4h@bahamas.gov.bs.

Bahamas National Patient Portal: System Overview

The Bahamas National Patient Portal is a secure, user-friendly online platform designed to enhance healthcare accessibility, patient engagement, and data management across the country’s public health system. Developed as part of the IS4H Bahamas initiative, the portal serves as a centralized hub where patients can access their health records, appointment schedules, test results, and medical history, all in one convenient location.

Key Features & Benefits

  • ✅ 24/7 Access to Health Records
  • ✅ Appointment Scheduling & Management
  • ✅ Secure Messaging & Notifications
  • ✅ Enhanced Data Security & Privacy
  • ✅ Seamless Integration with BahamasEMR+
  • ✅ Support for Telemedicine Services (upcoming)

Empowering Patients, Strengthening Healthcare
The Bahamas National Patient Portal is a transformational step toward modern, efficient, and patient-centered healthcare. By putting health information in the hands of patients, the system promotes greater engagement, better health outcomes, and improved public health services across The Bahamas.

For more information, contact is4h@bahamas.gov.bs.
