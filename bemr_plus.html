BahamasEMR+ – Features Guide

The BahamasEMR+ is a customized electronic medical records (EMR) system designed to modernize and streamline healthcare delivery in The Bahamas. As a localized version of OpenEMR, BahamasEMR+ ensures efficient patient data management, enhanced clinical workflows, and seamless coordination between healthcare providers—laying the foundation for a fully digitalized public health system.

Key Features of BahamasEMR+

  1. Centralized Patient Records
    • 🔹 One Patient, One Record: A comprehensive, unified health record accessible across healthcare facilities.
    • 🔹 Medical History Overview: Provides a full view of patient encounters, diagnoses, treatments, and medications.
    • 🔹 Allergy & Chronic Condition Tracking: Ensures critical patient information is always available for safe treatment.
  2. Streamlined Clinical Workflows
    • 🔹 Digital Documentation: Replaces paper-based records with structured electronic charts.
    • 🔹 Standardized Clinical Forms: Customizable templates for efficient documentation of patient visits.
    • 🔹 Automated Progress Notes: Reduces manual data entry, enabling faster and more accurate record-keeping.
  3. Appointment & Scheduling Management
    • 🔹 Integrated Booking System: Providers can schedule, reschedule, or cancel patient appointments with ease.
    • 🔹 Wait Time Reduction: Optimized scheduling helps reduce patient backlogs and improve clinic efficiency.
    • 🔹 Automated Appointment Reminders: Enhances patient attendance rates and reduces missed visits.
  4. Medication & Prescription Management
    • 🔹 Electronic Prescriptions (E-Prescribing): The next round of development of BEMR will enable safe, error-free prescription writing and pharmacy integration.
    • 🔹 Medication Reconciliation: Ensures accurate prescription tracking and refill management.
    • 🔹 Alerts & Warnings: Notifies providers of potential drug interactions or contraindications.
  5. Laboratory & Diagnostic Test Integration
    • 🔹 Lab Order Management: In the next phase of BEMR, physicians will be able to electronically request and track lab tests.
    • 🔹 Real-Time Test Results: Patients' test results will be digitally linked to their medical records for quick access.
    • 🔹 Diagnostic Imaging Integration: Supports uploading and reviewing X-rays, MRIs, and CT scans.
  6. Billing & Insurance Integration
    • 🔹 Automated Billing System: Through integration with DigiPay, BEMR will ensure efficient claims processing and payment tracking.
    • 🔹 Insurance Eligibility Checks: Verifies patient coverage in real-time.
    • 🔹 Customizable Fee Schedules: Aligns with government policies and private insurance frameworks.
  7. Secure & Compliant Data Management
    • 🔹 Role-Based Access Control: Ensures only authorized personnel can access sensitive health data.
    • 🔹 Data Protection Compliance: Implements industry-standard security protocols to safeguard patient information.
    • 🔹 Audit Trails: Tracks all system activities for accountability and quality control.
  8. Reporting & Analytics
    • 🔹 Customizable Dashboards: Through integration with Metabase, BEMR provides real-time insights into clinic operations and patient demographics.
    • 🔹 Health Trend Analysis: Enables evidence-based decision-making for public health initiatives.
    • 🔹 Regulatory & Compliance Reporting: Generates essential reports for government and healthcare agencies.

Benefits of BahamasEMR+

  • ✔️ Enhances Patient Care by ensuring health records are always up-to-date and accessible
  • ✔️ Reduces Administrative Burden through automated workflows and digital documentation
  • ✔️ Improves Clinical Efficiency by optimizing scheduling, prescribing, and patient tracking
  • ✔️ Strengthens Data Security with advanced encryption and access controls
  • ✔️ Supports Evidence-Based Healthcare through data-driven insights and analytics

The BahamasEMR+ is revolutionizing the way healthcare is delivered in The Bahamas. By replacing paper-based systems with an intuitive digital platform, BahamasEMR+ is enhancing patient outcomes, streamlining provider workflows, and ensuring seamless data management across the public health system.

For more information, contact is4h@bahamas.gov.bs.

BahamasEMR+ System Overview

BahamasEMR+ is the IS4H Bahamas electronic medical records (EMR) system, designed to streamline healthcare delivery, enhance clinical workflows, and improve patient outcomes across The Bahamas. As a localized and customized version of OpenEMR, BahamasEMR+ is tailored to meet the specific needs of the Bahamian public health system, ensuring efficiency, accuracy, and seamless integration of patient information.

Key Features & Benefits

  • ✅ Comprehensive Patient Records: Access to histories, medications, lab results, and plans in real time
  • ✅ Integrated Appointment Scheduling: Efficient registration, wait time reduction
  • ✅ Improved Clinical Documentation: Digital notes and prescriptions
  • ✅ Secure and Compliant Data Management: Only authorized access
  • ✅ Real-Time Decision Support: Alerts and analytics for informed care
  • ✅ Seamless Integration with the Bahamas National Patient Portal
  • ✅ Standardized Reporting & Data-Driven Insights: For health trends and planning

Revolutionizing Public Healthcare in The Bahamas
By digitizing patient records and streamlining clinical workflows, BahamasEMR+ is a game-changer for the nation’s healthcare system. It empowers healthcare professionals with real-time data, enhances efficiency, and ensures patients receive timely and coordinated care.
